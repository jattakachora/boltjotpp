# BoltJot

BoltJot is a note-taking app for Android by apphux. Capture a note straight from your lock screen, organise notes with labels and sub-labels, and add checklists, photos, drawings, voice notes and reminders.

Your notes are stored on your device. BoltJot has no accounts, no ads and no developer-run server.

## Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, BoltJot can back up your notes to a "BoltJot Backups" folder in your own Google Drive. It uses the `drive.file` permission, so it can only see the files it creates. Backups are encrypted on your device before upload. Full details are in the privacy policy.
